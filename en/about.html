About Our Family Business

ORGA_ME is a family-owned company originating from Togo and Senegal, established in 2022. For generations our family has gathered and prepared natural herbal products using traditional methods that prioritize quality, sustainability and the health of our customers. We specialize in organic teas and nutrient-rich powders made from locally sourced plants such as soursop, moringa and ginger. Our approach combines time-honored West African herbal knowledge with careful drying and packaging to preserve flavour and nutrients.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, organic plant-based products that nourish the body and support local communities, while advancing sustainable farming and preserving traditional herbal knowledge.
