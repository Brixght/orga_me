Tea Gingcuci Powder

ORGA_ME Tea Gingcuci Powder is a finely ground, ready-to-use herbal powder made from premium dried ginger root and select botanical ingredients sourced from smallholder farms in Togo and Senegal. The ginger is harvested at peak maturity, then carefully dried using low-temperature methods that preserve the volatile oils and active compounds responsible for its therapeutic properties. After drying, the ginger is milled into a fine powder and blended with complementary botanicals in precise proportions to create a balanced, harmonious flavor profile. This powder is crafted to dissolve easily into hot water, smoothies, or other beverages, delivering a fast and flavorful wellness boost without any preparation time. Its bright, spicy aroma and natural golden color make it a standout addition to any herbal routine, whether you are at home, at work, or traveling.

Benefits for the Body

Tea Gingcuci Powder delivers concentrated ginger compounds quickly for digestion, circulation, and immune support. The fine powder is rapidly absorbed for fast warming effects and immediate digestive comfort. The botanical blend works synergistically with ginger to amplify benefits, supporting balanced energy, muscle recovery, mental clarity, and overall wellness without caffeine.

How to Use

Mix one teaspoon of Tea Gingcuci Powder into 8 oz of hot water or your favorite beverage. Stir until fully dissolved and enjoy. Use daily. Can also be added to smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt, or iced beverages. Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place.

Ingredients

Ginger powder (Zingiber officinale), select botanical blend, natural herbal ingredients — no preservatives, no artificial additives.
