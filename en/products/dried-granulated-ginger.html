Dried Granulated Ginger

ORGA_ME Dried Granulated Ginger is made from ginger root harvested at peak maturity from smallholder farms in West Africa, then carefully washed, peeled, and sliced before undergoing a controlled low-temperature drying process. This method removes moisture while concentrating the natural gingerols and shogaols to a higher potency than sun-dried alternatives. After drying, the pieces are granulated into a coarse texture that retains active compounds while making the ginger easy to measure and use. The result is a more potent, aromatic product ideal for those seeking concentrated wellness support in a convenient, ready-to-use form.

Benefits for the Body

Dried granulated ginger supports digestion by encouraging the release of digestive enzymes and helping to ease feelings of fullness. As a warming spice, it is often used to bring comfort during cooler weather and to support healthy circulation. The controlled drying process concentrates beneficial compounds, making this form of ginger more potent than fresh ginger by weight for certain therapeutic applications.

This ginger is excellent for adding to teas, broths, or hot water to create a gentle warming drink. Many users find that a cup of dried granulated ginger tea in the morning helps wake up the digestive system and provides steady energy throughout the day. For immune support, ginger helps the body adapt to seasonal changes and supports the respiratory system.

In addition to digestive and circulation support, dried granulated ginger supports joint comfort by encouraging the body's natural response to occasional inflammation. It is also valued for its ability to enhance mood and mental clarity when used as part of a daily wellness routine. The granulated form allows for precise dosing, making it easy to incorporate into any daily routine.

How to Use

Add a teaspoon to hot water, tea, soups, sauces, or baked goods. Steep for 5-10 minutes to release full flavor and nutrients. Use once or twice daily for ongoing support. For a stronger infusion, increase the amount or steeping time according to your preference. The coarse texture means the granules will continue releasing flavor throughout the steeping process.

For a traditional ginger tea, combine one teaspoon of dried granulated ginger with 8 oz of boiling water. Cover and let steep for 7-10 minutes, then strain through a fine-mesh sieve. Add honey, lemon, or cinnamon to enhance the flavor. The tea can be enjoyed hot or chilled over ice for a refreshing summer beverage. Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
