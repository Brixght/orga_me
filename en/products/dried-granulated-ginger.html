Dried Granulated Ginger

ORGA_ME Dried Granulated Ginger is a specially processed ginger product designed for powerful flavor and lasting aroma. The ginger root is harvested at peak maturity from smallholder farms in West Africa, then carefully washed, peeled, and sliced into thin pieces before undergoing a controlled drying process. The drying removes moisture while concentrating the natural gingerols and shogaols that give ginger its characteristic warmth and health-supporting properties. After drying, the pieces are granulated into a coarse texture that retains active compounds while making the ginger easy to measure and use. This product is ideal for customers seeking the rich, earthy heat of ginger in a convenient, ready-to-use form that can be incorporated into both culinary and wellness routines. Each batch is quality-tested to ensure consistent potency and freshness.

How to Use

Add a teaspoon to hot water, tea, soups, sauces, or baked goods. Steep for 5-10 minutes to release full flavor and nutrients. Use once or twice daily for ongoing support. For a stronger infusion, increase the amount or steeping time according to your preference. The coarse texture means the granules will continue releasing flavor throughout the steeping process.

For a traditional ginger tea, combine one teaspoon of dried granulated ginger with 8 oz of boiling water. Cover and let steep for 7-10 minutes, then strain through a fine-mesh sieve. Add honey, lemon, or cinnamon to enhance the flavor. The tea can be enjoyed hot or chilled over ice for a refreshing summer beverage. Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
