Gingcuci — Ginger Blend

Classic Gingcuci

Gingcuci is our signature ginger blend — dried ginger root, zesty lemon peel, and natural granules slow-blended into a warming, bright infusion. Sourced from smallholder farms in Togo and Senegal, each batch is crafted for balanced flavor and therapeutic value in every cup.

Health Benefits & Body Support

Ginger, the cornerstone of Gingcuci, contains gingerols and shogaols that provide anti-inflammatory benefits throughout the body. Regular consumption supports digestion, soothes occasional nausea, and promotes circulation. The lemon component adds vitamin C and bioflavonoids for cardiovascular and immune support. Gingcuci also supports joint health, muscle recovery, and mental clarity by improving blood flow.

How to Prepare

Steep one heaping teaspoon of Gingcuci blend in 8 oz of hot water for 5-10 minutes. Strain and enjoy hot or iced. Add honey or natural sweeteners to taste. Consume once to twice daily.

Ingredients

Dried ginger root (Zingiber officinale), dried lemon peel, natural ginger granules — No additives, no preservatives, no artificial flavors.

Yellow Powdered Gingcuci

Yellow Powdered Gingcuci is a finely ground version of the classic blend, infused with turmeric-like botanicals for a warm golden color and boosted anti-inflammatory benefits. The fine powder dissolves instantly — perfect for teas, golden lattes, smoothies, and cooking.

Health Benefits & Body Support

The combination of ginger and yellow botanicals provides powerful anti-inflammatory synergy, supporting joint health, muscle recovery, and physical comfort. The curcuminoids offer antioxidant protection against oxidative stress, while gingerols support digestion, circulation, and immune function. This blend promotes detox pathways, liver function, and balanced energy, making it an excellent daily wellness addition.

How to Use

Mix one teaspoon into hot water or warm plant-based milk, stir until dissolved, and enjoy. Use once daily. For a golden latte, blend with warm milk and honey. Can also be added to smoothies, oatmeal, or yogurt.

Ingredients

Ginger root (Zingiber officinale), turmeric-like botanicals, natural yellow herbal blend — no preservatives, no artificial additives.
