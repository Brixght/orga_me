Classic Gingcuci

Gingcuci is our signature ginger blend — dried ginger root, zesty lemon peel, and natural granules slow-blended into a warming, bright infusion.

Health benefits

Gingerols and shogaols provide anti-inflammatory benefits. Regular consumption supports digestion, soothes occasional nausea, and promotes circulation. Lemon adds vitamin C and bioflavonoids for cardiovascular and immune support. Also supports joint health, muscle recovery and mental clarity.

How to prepare

Steep one heaping teaspoon in 8 oz hot water for 5–10 minutes. Strain, enjoy hot or iced. Add honey to taste. Once to twice daily.

Ingredients

Dried ginger root (Zingiber officinale), dried lemon peel, natural ginger granules — no additives, no preservatives.

Yellow Powdered Gingcuci

Finely ground classic blend infused with turmeric-like botanicals for a warm golden color and boosted anti-inflammatory benefits. Dissolves instantly — perfect for teas, golden lattes, smoothies and cooking.

Health benefits

Ginger + yellow botanicals create anti-inflammatory synergy for joint health, muscle recovery and comfort. Curcuminoids offer antioxidant protection; gingerols support digestion, circulation and immunity. Supports detox pathways, liver function and balanced energy.

How to use

Mix one teaspoon into hot water or warm plant-based milk. For a golden latte, blend with warm milk and honey. Also in smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt. Once daily.

Ingredients

Ginger root, turmeric-like botanicals, natural yellow herbal blend — no preservatives.
