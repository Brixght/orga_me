Classic Tea Gingcuci

Tea Gingcuci is a smooth herbal infusion blending ginger with traditional African botanicals for a warm, comforting cup that soothes and invigorates. Crafted for everyday wellness.

Benefits

Supports digestion, circulation and immune health. Warms the body, promotes circulation, eases tension and supports natural energy without caffeine. Aromatic compounds help reduce stress and support mental clarity, with gentle respiratory support and antioxidant protection.

How to use

Steep one teaspoon in 8 oz hot water for 5–8 minutes. Strain, enjoy warm or chilled. Once daily.

Ingredients

Ginger root, traditional botanicals, natural flavorings — no preservatives.

Tea Gingcuci Powder

Finely ground, ready-to-use herbal powder from premium ginger and select botanicals sourced in Togo and Senegal. Dissolves instantly in hot water, smoothies or any beverage.

Benefits

Concentrated ginger compounds for fast warming effects and digestive comfort. Botanical blend works synergistically with ginger — balanced energy, muscle recovery, mental clarity, overall wellness without caffeine.

How to use

Mix one teaspoon into 8 oz hot water or your favorite beverage. Stir until dissolved. Daily. Also in smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt or iced drinks.

Ingredients

Ginger powder (Zingiber officinale), select botanical blend — no preservatives.

Tea Gingcuci Yellow

Vibrant herbal powder blending ginger with turmeric-like botanicals for a warm, earthy cup with a golden hue. Finely ground for quick dissolution and maximum absorption.

Benefits

Anti-inflammatory synergy for joint health, muscle recovery and comfort. Supports detox pathways and liver function. Curcuminoids and bioflavonoids protect cells from oxidative stress — healthy aging, balanced energy.

How to use

One teaspoon in hot water, stir until dissolved. Once daily. For a golden latte, blend with warm plant-based milk and honey.

Ingredients

Ginger root (Zingiber officinale), herbal blend, natural yellow botanicals — no preservatives.
