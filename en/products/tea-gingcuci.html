Tea Gingcuci — Classic, Powder & Yellow

Classic Tea Gingcuci

Tea Gingcuci is a smooth herbal infusion blending ginger with traditional African botanicals for a warm, comforting cup that soothes and invigorates. Crafted for everyday wellness.

Tea Gingcuci supports digestion, circulation, and immune health through its ginger and botanical blend. It warms the body, promotes circulation, eases tension, and supports natural energy without caffeine. The aromatic compounds help reduce stress and support mental clarity. It also provides gentle respiratory support and antioxidant protection.

How to Use

Steep one teaspoon of Tea Gingcuci in 8 oz of hot water for 5-8 minutes. Strain and enjoy warm or chilled. Use once daily.

Ingredients

Ginger root, traditional botanicals, natural flavorings — no preservatives, no artificial additives.

Tea Gingcuci Powder

Tea Gingcuci Powder is a finely ground, ready-to-use herbal powder from premium ginger and select botanicals sourced in Togo and Senegal. It dissolves instantly in hot water, smoothies, or any beverage for a fast, flavorful wellness boost.

Tea Gingcuci Powder delivers concentrated ginger compounds quickly for digestion, circulation, and immune support. The fine powder is rapidly absorbed for fast warming effects and immediate digestive comfort. The botanical blend works synergistically with ginger to amplify benefits, supporting balanced energy, muscle recovery, mental clarity, and overall wellness without caffeine.

Mix one teaspoon of Tea Gingcuci Powder into 8 oz of hot water or your favorite beverage. Stir until fully dissolved and enjoy. Use daily. Can also be added to smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt, or iced beverages.

Ingredients

Ginger powder (Zingiber officinale), select botanical blend, natural herbal ingredients — no preservatives, no artificial additives.

Tea Gingcuci Yellow

Tea Gingcuci Yellow is a vibrant herbal powder blending ginger with turmeric-like botanicals for a warm, earthy cup with a golden hue. Finely ground for quick dissolution and maximum absorption of active compounds.
