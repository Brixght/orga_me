Soursop Powder

Our Soursop Powder is made from carefully selected, sun-dried soursop (Annona muricata) leaves, finely ground to create a vibrant green powder that captures all the nutritional wealth of this remarkable tropical plant. Sourced from smallholder farms committed to sustainable practices and processed with minimal handling, this powder retains the maximum concentration of vitamins, minerals, and phytonutrients.

Nutritional Powerhouse & Health Benefits

Soursop powder is nutrient-dense, containing vitamin A, C, E, iron, calcium, magnesium, and potassium. It contains powerful phytonutrients including acetogenins and polyphenols that support the immune system and protect cells from oxidative stress. It supports digestive health, promotes relaxation and calm, and aids the body's natural detoxification processes.

How to Prepare

Stir one teaspoon of soursop powder into warm water, tea, or juice until fully dissolved. Start with a smaller amount and increase as desired. Can also be added to smoothies, oatmeal, or other recipes. Take once to twice daily.

Ingredients

100% pure soursop leaf powder (Annona muricata) — No additives, no preservatives, no artificial flavors, no fillers.
