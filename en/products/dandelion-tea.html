Dandelion Tea

ORGA_ME Dandelion Tea is a gentle herbal infusion made from carefully selected dandelion leaves and roots harvested from wild-growing dandelion plants in the pristine fields of Togo and Senegal. The leaves and roots are hand-picked at their peak nutritional value, then cleaned and dried using traditional shade-drying techniques that preserve their green, earthy flavor and mild bitterness. Dandelion has long been valued in herbal medicine across Europe, Asia, and Africa for its cleansing qualities and its ability to support the liver, digestion, and overall detoxification. This tea is naturally caffeine-free and easy to enjoy every day, making it suitable for all ages. Each batch is carefully inspected to ensure only the finest leaves and roots make it into your cup.

Benefits for the Body

Dandelion tea supports healthy liver function and natural detoxification. It encourages bile flow for better digestion and nutrient absorption, supports kidney function and fluid balance, and provides vitamins A, C, K, potassium, and calcium. Its antioxidants protect cells from oxidative stress, and its mild bitterness stimulates digestive enzymes. Being caffeine-free, it is suitable anytime and also supports skin health, joint comfort, and healthy blood pressure.

How to Use

Steep one teaspoon of dandelion leaves and roots in hot water for 5-7 minutes. Strain and enjoy hot or chilled. Take once daily or after meals. Add honey and lemon to balance the earthy bitterness. Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place.

Ingredients

100% dried dandelion leaves and roots (Taraxacum officinale) — no additives, no preservatives, no artificial flavors.
