Granulated Ginger — Classic

ORGA_ME Granulated Ginger is coarsely granulated for a bold, warming flavor that releases gradually when steeped or cooked. Each batch is quality-tested for consistent aroma and potency.

Benefits for the body

Ginger supports digestion, circulation and immune health. It helps relieve mild nausea, supports healthy digestion, promotes warmth, and supports the body's natural inflammation response. Gingerols and shogaols protect cells from oxidative stress and contribute to improved energy, joint comfort and mental clarity.

How to use

Sprinkle a teaspoon into hot water, tea, soups, smoothies or baked goods. For tea: boil 8 oz water, add one teaspoon, simmer 5–7 minutes, strain, enjoy with honey or lemon. Use once or twice daily. Store cool and dry.

Ingredients

100% granulated ginger root (Zingiber officinale) — no additives, no preservatives, no artificial flavors.

Brownish Granulated Ginger

Dried slightly longer for a deeper, toasted flavor with warm, earthy undertones and mellower heat. Robust character shines in savory dishes, broths and winter preparations.

Benefits

Delivers gingerols and shogaols for digestion, circulation and immune support. Extended drying may concentrate certain compounds for a slightly more warming effect — joint comfort, digestive ease, natural energy.

How to use

One teaspoon per cup, steep 5–8 minutes. Excellent in soups, stews, marinades and baking. Once or twice daily.

Ingredients

100% brownish granulated ginger root (Zingiber officinale).

Drier Granulated Ginger

Low-temperature dried to concentrate gingerols and shogaols for a more potent, aromatic kick. Coarsely granulated for easy measuring.

Benefits

Encourages digestive enzymes and eases fullness. Warming properties promote healthy circulation. More potent than fresh ginger by weight — supports joint comfort, immune function and mental clarity.

How to use

One teaspoon in 8 oz boiling water, cover, steep 7–10 minutes, strain. Add honey, lemon or cinnamon. Once or twice daily.

Ingredients

100% dried granulated ginger root (Zingiber officinale).
