Granulated Ginger

ORGA_ME Granulated Ginger is a premium spice product made from dried ginger root that has been carefully granulated for convenience and strong flavor. Each batch is sourced from smallholder growers in Togo and Senegal who harvest the ginger at peak maturity during the late summer months when the root's aromatic compounds are most concentrated. The fresh ginger is peeled, sliced, and dried using traditional sun-drying methods that gently remove moisture while preserving the volatile essential oils and active compounds. After drying, the ginger is coarsely granulated to a texture that is coarser than powder, giving it a satisfying bite that releases ginger's sharp, comforting heat gradually when steeped or cooked. This format is ideal for blending into teas, soups, stews, marinades, and health tonics, offering versatility that powdered ginger cannot match. Each batch undergoes quality testing to ensure consistent flavor, aroma, and potency before being packaged in airtight containers.

Benefits for the Body

Ginger supports digestion, circulation, and immune health. Granulated ginger delivers these benefits in a convenient form that retains essential oils and active compounds. It helps relieve mild nausea, supports healthy digestion, promotes warmth, and supports the body's natural inflammation response. Gingerols and shogaols protect cells from oxidative stress and contribute to improved energy, joint comfort, and mental clarity.

How to Use

Sprinkle a teaspoon of granulated ginger into hot water, tea, soups, smoothies, or baked goods. For a simple ginger tea, boil 8 oz of water, add one teaspoon, simmer for 5-7 minutes, strain, and enjoy with honey or lemon. Use once or twice daily. Store in a cool, dry place.

Ingredients

100% granulated ginger root (Zingiber officinale) — no additives, no preservatives, no artificial flavors.

Brownish Granulated Ginger

Our Brownish Granulated Ginger offers a deeper, more complex flavor profile achieved through a slightly extended drying process that allows natural caramelization of the ginger's sugars. The result is a darker, richer granule with warm, earthy undertones and a mellower heat than standard granulated ginger. Sourced from the same smallholder farms in Togo and Senegal, this variant undergoes the same careful washing, peeling, and slicing before being dried at a controlled temperature that encourages gentle browning while preserving the essential oils and active compounds. The brownish granules bring a robust, almost toasted character to teas, broths, and spice blends, making them especially suited for savory cooking and hearty winter preparations. Each batch is tested to ensure consistent color, aroma, and potency.

Benefits for the Body

Like all our ginger products, the brownish variant delivers gingerols and shogaols for digestion, circulation, and immune support. The extended drying process may concentrate certain compounds, offering a slightly more warming effect. It supports joint comfort, digestive ease, and natural energy.

How to Use

Use one teaspoon per cup of hot water, steep for 5-8 minutes, strain and enjoy. Excellent in soups, stews, marinades, and baked goods for a deeper ginger flavor. Use once or twice daily.

Ingredients

100% brownish granulated ginger root (Zingiber officinale) — no additives, no preservatives.

Drier Granulated Ginger

Our Drier Granulated Ginger is made from ginger root harvested at peak maturity from smallholder farms in West Africa, then carefully washed, peeled, and sliced before undergoing a controlled low-temperature drying process. This method removes more moisture while concentrating the natural gingerols and shogaols to a higher potency than standard alternatives. After drying, the pieces are granulated into a coarse texture that retains active compounds while making the ginger easy to measure and use. The result is a more potent, aromatic product ideal for those seeking concentrated wellness support in a convenient, ready-to-use form.

Benefits for the Body

Dried granulated ginger supports digestion by encouraging digestive enzymes and easing fullness. Its warming properties promote healthy circulation and comfort during cooler weather. The controlled drying process concentrates gingerols and shogaols, making this form more potent than fresh ginger by weight. It supports joint comfort, immune function, and mental clarity as part of a daily wellness routine.

How to Use

Add a teaspoon to hot water, tea, soups, sauces, or baked goods. Steep for 5-10 minutes to release full flavor and nutrients. Use once or twice daily. For a traditional ginger tea, combine one teaspoon with 8 oz of boiling water, cover, steep for 7-10 minutes, then strain. Add honey, lemon, or cinnamon to taste.

Ingredients

100% dried granulated ginger root (Zingiber officinale) — no additives, no preservatives, no artificial flavors.
