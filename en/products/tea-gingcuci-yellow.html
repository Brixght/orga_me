How to Use

Mix one teaspoon into hot or warm water, stir until dissolved, and enjoy. Use once daily for ongoing support. For a richer experience, blend with warm plant-based milk and a touch of honey or maple syrup for a golden latte. The powder can also be added to smoothies, oatmeal, or yogurt for a colorful nutritional boost.

For an iced version, dissolve the powder in a small amount of hot water, then pour over ice and top with cold water or coconut water. The vibrant yellow color makes it a visually stunning beverage that is perfect for entertaining or special occasions. Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Due to the natural botanical pigments, the color may vary slightly between batches, which is a sign of authentic, minimally processed ingredients.
