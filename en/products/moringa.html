Nutritional powerhouse

One of the most nutrient-dense foods on the planet — vitamin A, C and E, plus iron, calcium, magnesium and potassium. All nine essential amino acids, powerful antioxidants like quercetin. Supports sustained energy, digestion, immune function, joint health and healthy skin.

How to use

Stir one teaspoon (5g) into smoothies, juices, soups, teas, oatmeal or yogurt. For best absorption of fat-soluble vitamins, consume with healthy fats like coconut oil or avocado. Once to twice daily.

Ingredients

100% pure moringa leaf powder (Moringa oleifera) — no additives, no fillers.
