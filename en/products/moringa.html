Our Moringa Powder is made from shade-dried moringa leaves (Moringa oleifera) ground to a fine, vibrant green powder that captures all the nutritional wealth of this remarkable plant. Nutritional Powerhouse & Body Support

Moringa is one of the most nutrient-dense foods on the planet, containing more than 90 nutrients in a single leaf. How to Use

Stir one teaspoon (about 5g) of moringa powder into your favorite smoothies, juices, soups, or teas for a nutrient boost. You can also sprinkle it on oatmeal, yogurt, salads, or other dishes. Start with a smaller amount and gradually increase as desired. For best results and optimal absorption of fat-soluble vitamins, consume with healthy fats such as coconut oil, nuts, or avocado. Take once to twice daily for optimal benefits. Due to its potency, use consistently for 2-4 weeks to experience noticeable improvements in energy, health, and vitality.
